# About LowDataSail

*Building the future of data-efficient maritime navigation*

---

## **🎯 Mission**

Our mission is to **democratize advanced sailing navigation** by developing **AI-powered, ultra-low-data solutions** that make **safety and performance** accessible to all sailors, regardless of their satellite bandwidth or budget.

---

## **🌍 Vision**

We envision a world where:

- **Every sailor** has access to **AI-powered navigation**
- **Satellite costs** are no longer a barrier to **safe offshore sailing**
- **Extreme events** are **predicted and avoided** before they become dangerous
- **Edge devices** (like Raspberry Pi) can run **sophisticated routing algorithms**
- **Open-source** collaboration drives **innovation in maritime technology**

## **💼 Business Model**

### **Open Core Model**
- **Core Features:** Free and open-source (MIT License)
- **Premium Features:** Available via subscription
- **Enterprise Features:** Custom solutions for commercial users

### **Revenue Streams**
1. **Subscriptions:** Monthly/annual fees for premium features
2. **Consulting:** Custom development and integration services
3. **Hardware Bundles:** Pre-configured Raspberry Pi bundles
4. **Data Services:** Premium weather data access
5. **White-Label:** Licensing our technology to other companies

### **Pricing Philosophy**
- **Free for individuals** and small teams
- **Affordable for professionals** and racing teams
- **Cost-effective for enterprises** and commercial shipping
