# Market Positioning Analysis

*Strategic positioning for Light Router in the sailing routing market*

---

## **🎯 Positioning Statement**

**For** offshore sailors, racers, and research vessels **who** need reliable routing in satellite-constrained environments, **Light Router** is the **AI-powered, ultra-low-data sailing router** that **delivers competitive route quality and superior extreme event prediction** while using **100-1000x less data** than existing solutions.

**Unlike** traditional routers that prioritize features over efficiency, **Light Router** is **purpose-built** for the **real constraints** of offshore sailing: **limited bandwidth, edge hardware, and extreme safety requirements**.

  - **Satellite costs:** $15-150/day for weather data
  - **Bandwidth limitations:** Iridium (2-5 KB/s), Starlink (50-100 KB/s maritime)

  - **Safety requirements:** Must avoid hazards at all costs

- **Current Solutions:**
  - PredictWind ($30-50/month)
  - StormGeo ($1000+/month)
  - SailGrib WR ($10-20/month)
  - qtVlm (Free)

  - **Performance:** Competitive with top tools
  - **Safety:** Superior extreme event prediction

- **Willingness to Pay:** **High** ($50-200/month)

- **Size:** **500-1,000 teams** worldwide

- **Channels:**

## **💰 Pricing Strategy**

### **Pricing Model**

| **Model** | **Description** | **Pros** | **Cons** | **Best For** |
|----------|-----------------|----------|----------|-------------|
| **Freemium** | Free for basic, paid for advanced | Easy adoption, viral growth | Hard to monetize | Individuals, small teams |
| **Subscription** | Monthly/annual fee | Predictable revenue, scalable | Churn risk | All users |
| **Per-Use** | Pay per route calculation | Aligns with value | Complex to implement | Commercial users |
| **Enterprise** | Custom pricing for fleets | High revenue, long-term contracts | Complex sales | Commercial shipping |
| **Open Core** | Free core, paid features | Community adoption, transparency | Hard to balance | Developers, enterprises |

**Recommended Model:** **Freemium + Subscription + Enterprise**

### **Pricing Tiers**

#### **Free Tier** *(For individuals and small teams)*
- **Features:**
  - Basic routing
  - <100nm routes
  - 6h forecast updates
  - Community support
- **Limitations:**
  - 5 routes/day
  - No extreme event prediction
  - No advanced optimization
  - No priority support
- **Target:** Individuals, small boats, trial users

#### **Pro Tier** *($10-20/month)* *(For serious sailors)*
- **Everything in Free, plus:**
  - Unlimited routes
  - Extreme event prediction (storms, icebergs)
  - 24h forecast updates
  - Advanced optimization
  - Email support
- **Target:** Offshore cruisers, amateur racers

#### **Team Tier** *($50-100/month)* *(For racing teams)*
- **Everything in Pro, plus:**
  - Unlimited routes for team
  - Rogue wave prediction
  - Microburst detection
  - 48h forecast updates
  - Priority support
  - Team collaboration features
- **Target:** Professional racing teams, research vessels

#### **Enterprise Tier** *(Custom pricing)* *(For fleets)*
- **Everything in Team, plus:**
  - Fleet management dashboard
  - Centralized routing
  - API access
  - Custom integration
  - Dedicated support
  - SLA guarantees
- **Target:** Commercial shipping, large research organizations
